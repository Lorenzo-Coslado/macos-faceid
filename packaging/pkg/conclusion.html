Almost there — one step left.

Open Mugshot from your Applications folder and register your face. macOS will ask for camera access; that is the only permission left to grant.

Then try it:

sudo -k && sudo true

To remove Mugshot later, open it and choose Uninstall Mugshot… at the bottom of its window. That undoes the sudo rule and restores the system Touch ID rule — dragging the app to the Trash on its own would leave both behind.
