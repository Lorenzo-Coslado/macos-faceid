Finish setup in Mugshot.

Mugshot opens automatically in your logged-in session. If no window appears, open Mugshot from Applications. Allow the camera and register your face.

The same setup then offers optional lock-screen unlock: enable Mugshot in Privacy & Security › Accessibility and enter your Mac login password. Mugshot detects the permission and continues automatically. You can choose Set up later. macOS requires your consent for camera and Accessibility; the installer cannot grant these permissions for you.

If you selected Enable Face ID for sudo, try it in a terminal:

sudo -k && sudo true

To remove Mugshot later, open it and choose Uninstall Mugshot… at the bottom of its window. That undoes the sudo rule and restores the system Touch ID rule; dragging the app to the Trash would leave both behind.
