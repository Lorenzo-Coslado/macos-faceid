Mugshot unlocks sudo with your face. Recognition runs entirely on this Mac, and nothing ever leaves it.

This installer asks for your password once, and uses it to put Mugshot in your Applications folder and to wire sudo to face unlock. Installing by hand instead costs two separate macOS permissions.

On the next screen you can clear Enable Face ID for sudo if you would rather decide later — the app can turn it on itself afterwards.

You cannot get locked out. If recognition fails, if the app is closed, or if you delete it, sudo asks for your password exactly as before.

Not a security product. A 2D webcam can be fooled by a photo. Keep Touch ID or your password as your real protection.
