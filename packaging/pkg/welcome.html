Mugshot unlocks sudo with your face. Recognition runs entirely on this Mac, and nothing ever leaves it.

The installer asks for your password once, to place the app in Applications and — if you keep the option selected on the next screen — to write the sudo rule. Doing that from the app instead would require you to grant two separate macOS permissions by hand.

You cannot get locked out. The rule is sufficient: if recognition fails, if the app is closed, or if you delete it, sudo simply asks for your password as before.

Not a security product. A 2D webcam can be fooled by a photo. Keep Touch ID or your password as your real protection.
